# Penny flagship plan: from customer evidence to verified resolution

**Status: proposed direction beyond Penny's current 0.0.0 source candidate.** The current source supports verified requester submission, voting, following, moderated comments and edits, operator publication, status updates, and requester notifications. It also includes maintained Base operator authentication, operator evidence and decision writes, approved roadmap changes, and release publishing. The broader flagship direction remains proposed. The bounded Stage 2–3 implementation noted below adds source-cited AI and selected Orbit evidence refresh; connected delivery, measured customer outcomes and the real pilot gates remain open. Current behavior and open proof boundaries are recorded in [acceptance](ACCEPTANCE.md), [the read-spine map](docs/read-spine.md), and [the migration record](docs/start-migration.md). Future acceptance gates are in [flagship acceptance and experiments](docs/flagship-acceptance.md); [the frontier extension](docs/frontier-extension.md) and [user journeys](docs/user-journeys.md) describe the intended destination.

## The product and its first wedge

Penny should become a continuously operating customer-learning team for one B2B software business. Its job is to help the product lead resolve consequential recurring customer problems, then establish what happened after the business acted. Its unit of work is an unresolved question, not a request card, AI summary, or priority score.

The first wedge is **recurring friction that may contribute to avoidable cancellation**. A cancellation explanation can reveal an important problem, but people who cancel are a selected group. Penny must preserve that context and compare it with other segments, support and sales evidence, observed workarounds, and product behavior before treating it as a wider demand signal. The product should also be willing to conclude that a problem calls for better onboarding, a support or operating change, pricing or packaging work, a different product change, or no new intervention. The owner states a goal when they have one; when they do not, Penny proposes a correctable goal from the first authorized example rather than requiring a blank prompt or setup exercise.

The primary user is the product lead in a B2B software company who owns an investment decision across product, support, success, and sales evidence. Those teams can contribute evidence and carry out actions; they do not turn Penny into a support inbox, CRM, or delivery tracker. The important customer job is to get an answer to “did this address the problem I described?” rather than merely receive a shipped notification.

Consider a hypothetical month-end bookkeeping problem. “Email my accountant a CSV,” “let my accountant access the records,” and “integrate with our accounting system” are three possible solutions to the same broad job—less recurring work to close the books—not three interchangeable feature requests. A product lead may find the actual need is discoverability, a missing export, a handoff workflow, or an unrelated setup problem. Penny should help investigate that distinction. Any figures, people, or quotes in a design exercise are fictional until they are tied to real, authorized source records; the existing curated sample is not customer research.

## The complete learning journey

1. **Capture.** Use selected, permitted portal, support, sales, cancellation, interview, or product-usage sources. Preserve original content, source/time, consent or use basis, explicit identity/account links, and event IDs; replays must be idempotent.
2. **Interpret.** Keep source facts separate from versioned interpretations citing exact spans, contradictions, repetition, uncertainty and missing segments. Distinguish people, accounts and mentions; model confidence never establishes identity.
3. **Investigate.** Agents find decision-relevant gaps and perform useful scoped work: inspect permitted sources, compare segments/workarounds, propose a clarifying question or test, query an allowed cohort, or prepare a prototype. Each investigation records why the evidence matters, what could change the view, likely cost/owner attention and scope limits. Recurring agents should acquire evidence and advance work, not just summarize or add backlog.
4. **Compare and decide.** Compare product, onboarding, support/process, pricing, bounded test and no-build options on common evidence, cohort, counter-evidence, mechanism, delivery, outcome and uncertainty dimensions. Penny prepares the brief and pre-fills known owner, rationale, confidence, review date and evidence snapshot fields; the owner supplies only a meaningful choice or correction. Its reasoned recommendation is advice about a useful next action, never a computed bet ranking. Options remain comparable under `DEC-003` without a combined score or silent public commitment.
5. **Deliver and learn.** Link the choice to owned tracker/agent work; agents may prepare artifacts and advance work within scope while the tracker remains authoritative for its issue. Penny prepares the cohort, baseline, measure, window, data source, comparison, stop rule and spend envelope before a consequential experiment. It runs the already-authorized steps, asks once for any missing effect grant, and keeps independent in-scope work moving if that effect is held. Connect real delivery and outcome evidence, label mixed/negative/missing results, and feed new versioned observations into later decisions. Shipping before renewal does not prove retention impact.

## Accepted experience

Keep the **decision-first** work model: every consequential question connects source evidence and gaps, contested problem interpretation, comparable interventions/accountable decision, and delivery/outcome status. The [user journeys](docs/user-journeys.md) define its quiet home: **Results** (including negative, null and incomplete findings), **Learning** (the current question, material change and next check), and **Needs you** (only a meaningful decision, authority boundary, consent issue or exception). A result never labels an unsolved customer problem as solved because code shipped, a test passed or analysis completed. When nothing material changed, Penny says so once and continues in scope.

Only **Needs you** creates a concise actionable card: Penny's reasoned recommendation, why now, supporting and contradicting evidence, known uncertainty, a real preview, consequence and cost, and **Do this**, **Change it** and **Not now** controls. Results and Learning stay informational, with optional inspect/correct/pause actions; they do not become a stream of review tasks. A recommendation is an advisory case, not an automatic ranking; the alternatives remain side by side on the common dimensions in `DEC-003`. Evidence spans and technical detail appear on deliberate drill-down. The extension's semantic choices, models, taxonomies and agents remain internal implementation details, not setup screens. Use the existing Penny workspace, with discoverable pause-one/pause-all, action and cost inspection, correction, and source-disconnect controls.

The owner sets direction and a narrow source/effect grant once. Penny proposes a goal from the first permitted evidence item when none exists, prepares mappings and measurement fields, and proceeds through reversible or otherwise authorized work without asking for each internal step. It asks only when a choice changes strategy or an effect crosses source, spend, contact, publication or production authority. Silence never widens a grant; affected work stops at the boundary, while independent work already in scope continues. Routine imports, checks and unchanged progress send no push. The owner may opt into one weekly digest of material changes. A consequential card creates one notice and receives at most one reminder before its declared deadline or grant expiry; at expiry Penny stops only the affected effect, with other in-scope work continuing.

Show investigation progress as work: sources examined, a useful clarification/test, a prototype or brief, an escalated exception, delivery receipt, or evaluated outcome. Do not optimize insight counts or force review of every reversible internal action; surface uncertainty and exceptions that could change the decision.

Public feedback, roadmap, releases and customer follow-up are deliberate, customer-safe projections, not the evidence store or proof of success. Penny needs a useful operator UI; APIs, agents and MCP extend that experience rather than replace it.

## Evidence model and ownership boundaries

Keep explicit records for connection/scope; source observation or event ID; identity assertion and basis; cited, versioned interpretation; investigation protocol/gap/actions/cost; alternatives; decision/evidence snapshot; delivery link/receipts; and outcome protocol/observations. Original input, AI interpretation, accepted decision and observed result remain separate histories, with consent basis and allow-listed public projections. Identity and account fields stay nullable unless supplied by the recorder or source; never ask a person to resolve a non-consequential unknown just to complete a record. Preserve immutable decision/outcome snapshots, version corrections, honor erasure through derived records, and make source replay idempotent.

Deletion, correction and revocation must propagate through copies, indexes, embeddings, artifacts, notification snapshots, exports and restores. Replays never duplicate evidence or contact. Revocation stops future access and identifies derived records for retraction, erasure or governed anonymization. Portable export preserves the source-to-outcome history.

Keep each product's source records in the product that owns them:

- **HappyToHelp** owns conversations and support workflow; a future connector may pass selected, permitted evidence. No connection exists; live `app.happytohelp.com` is untouched.
- **Orbit** owns CRM identity/opportunities and sales action. Its signed/idempotent, exact-key and unlinked-review patterns are references, not a Penny integration or product-priority algorithm. Never fuzzy-link a sales record.
- **Delivery trackers** own issues/projects; Penny owns rationale and measurement, exchanging only scoped links, work commands and receipts.
- **Analytics providers** own event collection; Penny uses only authorized cohorts and reports join/causal limits.

Providers remain replaceable; no shared database or universal customer graph is required. Each selected connector defines API, keys, ownership, replay, partial failure, rate limits and erasure. Models may suggest identity links for review only. Wrong output is corrected through instructions, context, normalization, schema, model or tools—not semantic fallback or unbounded retry.

## Differentiation and falsification

The market review shows that AI extraction, source-linked idea records, clarifying questions, scoring, MCP, product-specification workflows, tracker handoff, and post-launch evaluation are already represented in current vendor products, with different availability and maturity. Penny must not claim it invented those features, that incumbents lack AI, or that every competitor is obsolete. The hypothesis worth testing is narrower and harder: can one owner-adaptable system lower the total work, cost, elapsed time, and owner attention to reach a **verified useful action** on an important problem, while preserving attribution, consent, decision accountability, and outcome limits?

Test that claim on equivalent real cases against the best configured current workflow (including Canny, Linear, or Productboard when they fit), an agent using those existing tools, and scratch or open-source construction where relevant. Count all setup/integration, every unsuccessful attempt, model and provider spend, human review, delays, reversals, and later updates. Measure active attention for every human—including owner, staff and participating customers—across setup, source correction, approval, investigation, decision, follow-up and recovery; report machine wait and wall time separately. Record notifications, repeated asks, correction time, customer effort and any recovery the owner had to perform. Set acceptable attention, interruption, contact and correction thresholds before exposing pilot results; these are targets, not measured performance. Compare evidence completeness, false associations and their recovery, time to a defensible decision and to action, outcome coverage, and total attention. Measure whether an agent actually performs useful investigation and changes the work, not whether it generates a persuasive brief. If another product plus an agent reaches the same quality floor with less total cost and attention, narrow Penny, integrate with that product, or abandon this direction. The [acceptance plan](docs/flagship-acceptance.md) names the protocols and what evidence is needed before drawing that conclusion.

## Product sequence

For Penny implementation, the sequence remains TanStack Start migration and parked-source reconciliation, then a trustworthy native write path, one complete real learning pilot, continuous scoped investigations, delivery/outcome evidence, and broader workflows only where pilots justify them. The [frontier extension](docs/frontier-extension.md) adds fast semantic awareness, frontier investigation and implementation teams, and real outcome tests under explicit grants. Its hypotheses can be tested earlier in isolated, authorized experiments; the main implementation still starts with the migration and need not build every baseline feature before learning whether this direction works. Detailed dependencies, checks, and stop/advance decisions are in [docs/flagship-acceptance.md](docs/flagship-acceptance.md). This remains a planning target, not release authority.
