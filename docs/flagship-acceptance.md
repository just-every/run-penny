# Penny flagship delivery and acceptance plan

**Planning document only.** These are future stage gates for the [flagship direction](../FLAGSHIP-PLAN.md), not current Penny acceptance, tests, product guarantees, or release status. Every measurement target, threshold, cohort, and permitted action must be recorded before the relevant pilot. Never count curated sample data, generated examples, a green build, or a source publication as customer or product outcome evidence.

The intended destination remains a continuous investigation and learning system. Stages establish prerequisites and evidence; passing the first pilot does not shrink or complete that destination. Any implementation resumes from the required TanStack Start conversion, then reconciles the parked source with main. Competitive research remains private and is not distributed with the product.

**Stage 0 implementation evidence, 29 September 2026:** [Runtime conversion and source reconciliation](start-migration.md). This records local proof only and leaves the later stage gates intact.

## Proposed user-effort and interruption criteria

These are future acceptance measurements, not current performance. Before a pilot sees results, record an acceptable budget and pass threshold for each audience and workflow; report target values separately from observations. Apply these measures to the full path from first setup to a verified outcome, including failed attempts.

| Area | Measure and planned product criterion |
| --- | --- |
| **Human attention** | Record active minutes for every human affected: owner/operators, staff contributors, and participating customers. Include source selection and setup, mapping review, evidence correction, approval, research participation, decisions, follow-up and recovery. Report setup-to-first-value, recurring effort per useful action and total minutes separately from machine wait and wall time. |
| **Notifications and silence** | Count each push, card, digest and reminder by recipient and cause. Routine imports, tests and unchanged progress send no push. A material choice creates one card/notice and at most one reminder before its recorded deadline or grant expiry; an owner may opt into one weekly material-change digest. Nonresponse never expands scope; at expiry stop only the affected effect and continue independent valid work. Measure the share of notices that required action and any repeated asks for the same choice; target zero duplicate asks. |
| **Customer effort and consent** | Count invitations and questions by purpose, channel, recipient and time window, plus responses, refusals, nonresponse and active customer minutes. A follow-up requires both recipient purpose consent and the matching source/channel grant; default to one useful question per open gap, no cross-channel retry after silence, and a predeclared contact ceiling. Target zero duplicate or unauthorized contact. Votes and email addresses do not count as consent. |
| **Correction and recovery** | Measure active minutes, number of corrections, residual false links, affected decision cards, and owner actions needed to repair a mapping, correct an interpretation, reconnect a source, resume safely or recover a partial effect. The product should carry a simple correction through its derived records and show any remaining pause; the owner should not need to inspect logs or manually repair indexes. Set an acceptable residual-error and correction-time threshold before the pilot. |

The initial grant should state the source, purpose, effect classes, cost/contact ceiling, expiry and stop behavior in plain language. Penny pre-fills known measurement and execution fields. Reviewers must record which steps completed with no new owner decision and which meaningful choices were required; approval of every internal step is a failure of the low-effort hypothesis. All targets remain hypotheses until a real pilot measures them.

## Named research and evaluation protocols

Use real, authorized records; a protocol description is not proof it has been run. Fix the population, data window, metrics, allowable actions, and interpretation rules before viewing the result. Keep only the identifiers required by that protocol.

Every connector evaluation records whether each source item is visible to all equal operators under `OPS-001`. A source record with narrower provider access must be filtered before it enters Penny, unless a separately reviewed and tested contract change adds narrower access within Penny. Test provider replay, credential revocation, partial outage and erasure across original copies and derived artifacts. `REQ-010` means a vote or an operator-entered email is not consent to contact. `REL-004` preserves the exact target/recipient context at publication; `NOTIFY-002` rechecks preference, suppression and unsubscribe when sending; `REL-003` requires a deliberate version for correction sends. `NOTIFY-004` still forbids inbound email replies creating Penny records. New customer research has its own explicit purpose and permission.

### 1. Cancellation Context Capture

For consenting participating accounts, capture the customer's stated reason, the task they were attempting, the steps and workarounds tried, the moment and channel, the customer's own proposed remedy, what would have changed their decision, and whether a response is permitted. Ask one useful follow-up only when its answer can distinguish plausible explanations. Classify missing answers and refusals rather than filling them with model inference. Compare cancellation evidence with non-canceling customers and other source types before generalizing. The protocol tests whether the system gets decision-relevant evidence at a moment when existing records are thin; it does not assume a feature can or did prevent a cancellation.

### 2. Problem and Identity Fidelity Challenge

Before agents see the evaluation set, independent reviewers label the minimal set of real statements with their source span, source identity, explicitly known account, candidate problem, plausible alternative interpretation, and unresolved fields. Include repeated mentions from the same account, false friends, contradictory statements, unlabeled identity, and records from cancellation and non-cancellation contexts. Compare proposed links, problem groupings, contradictions, uncertainty and abstentions with the reviewed set. Record false associations, missed relationships, reversal time, and retained original context. Choose acceptable error thresholds according to the impact of the action before running; do not turn agreement with an AI-generated label into ground truth.

### 3. Missing-Evidence Yield

For each open decision, define in advance what evidence could change it. Penny's agent proposes a cohort and method, explains why that answer matters, estimates cost/attention, and respects source scope and customer consent. In evaluation, an independent reviewer (not the product owner doing routine work) scores whether the inquiry reaches the relevant segment and distinguishes the competing explanations. During ordinary operation, Penny follows the approved inquiry protocol and standing channel grant; it asks the owner only if contact authority or a consequential change is missing. Track completion, response, refusal, nonresponse and what decision field changed. Compare with the current manual process and the best available incumbent workflow. An unreturned message is not a negative answer; a large set of low-value suggested questions fails this protocol.

### 4. Intervention Outcome Protocol

Before a feature, onboarding, support, policy or other intervention, record the target problem and cohort, operational definition of success, baseline, observation window, event/source, relevant exclusions, and comparison. Prefer randomized or staged comparisons only where they are ethical and feasible; otherwise use an explicit observational or matched-cohort method and state confounders. At review, include adoption, customer response, problem movement, retention/cancellation observations where relevant, nulls, adverse effects, and missingness. Separate “accounts representing revenue” from “revenue affected” and both from “revenue saved.” State a causal conclusion only to the strength of the design. Preserve unsuccessful and unresolved outcomes as learning, not system errors to be hidden.

### 5. Time to Verified Useful Action and Total Cost

Use comparable real decision cases, a shared quality rubric, and balanced task order. Where the owner has access, compare Penny with a best-configured incumbent path (including Canny, Linear, or Productboard as fitted), an agent operating those tools, and an open-source or scratch-build alternative. Record exact product version, plan, enabled capability, and data/connectors for the trial; feature labels and maturity can change. Measure from evidence availability to an owner-accepted, useful action and its verified outcome—not to the first plausible answer. Count every failed attempt, setup/integration, agent and model cost, provider charges, human investigation/review, elapsed time, correction/reversal, follow-up, and ongoing maintenance. Record what an owner had to decide or do. Set thresholds before the run. If Penny's workflow does not improve quality-adjusted work/cost/elapsed time/attention, the result is evidence against the product hypothesis, not an invitation to reinterpret the scoring.

For consistency, report the following with each case and the raw numerator, denominator, time window and source:

| Measure | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| **Distinct-account coverage** | Number of explicitly linked, distinct accounts with at least one in-scope source observation, divided by the number of accounts in the predeclared target cohort. Report unknown/unlinked observations separately; never count people or repeated mentions as accounts. |
| **Mention frequency** | Source observations or distinct source events that support the problem, with repeated events from one account visible. It is not a customer count. |
| **Attribution error** | Among independently reviewed identity/problem links, count confirmed incorrect links and missed links separately, each divided by the number of reviewed applicable links. Also report abstentions and the exact resolution basis. Do not hide uncertainty in one accuracy score. |
| **Outcome coverage** | For a self-report protocol, distinct eligible recipients with a valid response divided by the immutable eligible recipient snapshot; show nonresponse and suppression separately. For telemetry, predeclare a different cohort denominator and report cohort members with complete baseline/follow-up observations divided by eligible cohort members. Never combine the two denominators. |
| **Outcome rate** | Count of cohort members meeting the predeclared observable outcome divided by the relevant eligible cohort denominator, with numerator, denominator, baseline, follow-up window and missingness shown. A respondent-only rate must be labeled as such. |
| **Human attention** | Active human minutes spent by every affected person—including owners/operators, staff and customer participants—on setup, capture, source correction, review, investigation, decision, participation, follow-up, corrections and recovery, reported per accepted action and in total. Keep machine wait time separate; report wall elapsed time from evidence availability to accepted action and to verified outcome separately. |
| **Cost to verified useful action** | Actual model/provider spend plus attributable implementation and operating cost, alongside human minutes and wall time, per action that met the predeclared quality rubric and reached its verification point. Include failed attempts and reversals in totals. |
