- A saved-import erasure too large for one request no longer refuses: a record in a retained plan over 512 KiB is erased through Base's staged erasure form, and its private stage continues across resume requests until it publishes (with the same journal receipt and restore checkpoint), so a legacy plan near the 24 MiB import limit erases in bounded steps while everyone else's history keeps undo, resume and restore.
